What I build, and how

Straightforward web development for small businesses, no jargon, no overselling. Here's exactly what's included and what things cost.

01

Business Websites

A clean, fast, mobile ready website built around what your business actually needs, not a generic template. Includes a homepage, service or product pages, and a way for people to contact you, built to represent your business well and turn visitors into inquiries.

02

SEO Foundations

Every site I build starts with proper structure, page speed, and metadata already in place, page titles, descriptions, image alt text, sitemap. This is the difference between a site that just exists, and one Google can actually find and rank for the searches your customers are making.

03

Firebase Powered Features

Bookings, client dashboards, real time updates, without needing a heavyweight traditional backend. If your business needs more than a static site, appointment requests, a login area, live availability, this is where it gets built in.

04

Care & Maintenance

Ongoing updates, fixes, and monitoring after launch. Most small business sites break quietly over time, broken links, outdated info, no one notices until a customer does. This keeps your site working without you having to think about it.
